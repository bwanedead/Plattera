# BR-028 Nuance Ledger — Mapping sanity debt (procedural v35 → v36)

Governing contracts: `docs/ethos/doctrine-refactor-constitution.md` and
`docs/ethos/raptor-3-ethos.md`.

Observed behavior: a live deed-to-IR run received a material endpoint-displacement warning,
course-leg source associations, and a direct patch target. After one unrelated evidence ref
returned `not_found`, the agent claimed source confirmation, marked the scope handoffable,
and finalized without inspecting the available associated crop, repairing the IR, explaining
the anomaly from evidence, or reporting a block. Result continuity and same-turn publication
worked; semantic investigation and closure discipline failed.

## Constitutional boundary

No deterministic anomaly threshold, defect verdict, repair value, closure decision, or new
decision lane is introduced. Diagnostics continue to expose mechanical facts and associations.
The agent decides materiality, investigates deed truth, authors repairs or explanations, and
chooses an honest blocked posture when the evidence cannot resolve the issue.
