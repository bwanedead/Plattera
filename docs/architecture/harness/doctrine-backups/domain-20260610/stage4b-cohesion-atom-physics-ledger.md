## Derivable-deletion judgment (constitution §7.1)

§7.1 encourages the narrative to fund rule deletions. Decision: **zero deletions in 4b.**
The deletion candidates on this surface are calibrated rhythm/rationale sentences
(placeholder rationale, pin rationale, packet economics) that are (a) test-pinned, (b) the
most sensitive text in the system per §8 staging, and (c) operational hows rather than
restatements of the chain's whys. Cutting calibrated text against the wording-is-weights law
to satisfy a deletion quota would invert the constitution's priority order. True echoes are
handled in Stage 4c; future incident-driven edits should tune the narrative first
(tune-don't-append).
